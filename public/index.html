Meet CoAnalyst 360.
AI that drives investigations forward.

A persistent, multi-agent system built into the fabric of the Penlink platform. CoAnalyst 360 understands investigative intent, orchestrates workflows across open-source intel, evidentiary data, and live communications, and maintains context from first query to final outcome. 

  • ✓ Persistent context — maintain a single thread of analysis across the full case lifecycle, without manual re-connection.
